# COMETS Accreditation Documents

This repository contains the accreditation self-study and PCSAS review 
response for the Clinical Psychological Science concentration at The 
University of Texas at Dallas, shared in support of the manuscript:

Callahan, J. L., Ruggero, C. J., Warren, S. L., Kent, J. S., 
Pinkham, A. E., Kakhnovets Ybarra, R., & Holub, S. C. (under review). 
Rebuilding clinical science training: An evidence-first framework for 
structural reform. *Clinical Psychological Science*.

## Contents

- COMETS Accreditation Self-Study (with selective redactions)
- PCSAS Review Response (unredacted)

## Redaction Policy

The self-study is shared in near-complete form. Appendices have been 
redacted where content was: (1) irrelevant to the COMETS framework, 
(2) owned by external parties, or (3) identified individuals beyond 
what is already available on departmental websites. The full 
appendix list is retained with redacted items clearly marked.

Appendices retained in full include the curriculum map, concentration 
handbook, practicum handbook, doctoral and internship level competency 
codebooks, and alumni survey plan.
